About me

Hi! I'm João, an English teacher in Cuiabá, Mato Grosso. I teach at Colégio São Benedito, in middle and high school, and in a bilingual program for young children.

How I teach

I believe people learn better when they play. That's why I use games and stories in my classes, and I adapt each lesson to the level of the class, with simple words and small steps.

How I made this site

I designed this site and chose every detail, from the ASCII beach to the fishing dwarf, inspired by the game Dwarf Fortress. The code was written with help from Claude, an AI assistant.
