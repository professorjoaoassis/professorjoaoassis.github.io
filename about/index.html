About me

Who I am

I'm João Pedro de Assis, an English teacher in Cuiabá, Mato Grosso. I teach children, teenagers and adults, and I create educational solutions and digital tools for learning English.

My view on teaching

I believe that learning a language goes far beyond memorizing rules and words. A new language changes the way we communicate, how we position ourselves and how we connect with the world.

With a new language, you can reach information, cultures and points of view that once seemed far away. The language stops being a barrier and becomes a tool for expression and personal growth.

That is where the solutions and tools I create come from: when students try, make mistakes and try again, English stops being just a school subject and truly becomes theirs. Above all, learning a language is a way to strengthen your own voice in the world.
