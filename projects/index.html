Cuiabá heat map

map

An interactive map that shows the hottest parts of Cuiabá and Várzea Grande using satellite images, with layers for trees, buildings and how the heat has changed since the 1980s. The next step adds MapBiomas land-use data from 1985 to today, so we can compare where the city grew and where nature disappeared with where it is hottest now. In class, environmental education becomes an investigation: students find their home and school on the map, ask why some neighborhoods are hotter, and suggest where to plant trees, using real data from their own city.

School management system

tool

A system to manage a school's academic records. Teachers and coordinators sign in with their own accounts, each with the right access. Teachers enter grades and absences; the system calculates the averages, shows who needs extra support and builds the report cards, ready to export as PDF or Excel. It also keeps class lists, student records and the team's lesson plans in one place.

Lesson guide · middle and high school

tool

A planner for the whole school term, lesson by lesson: topic, book pages, homework and a plan B for students without their material. It reminds me which homework to check in each class, records homework for each student, tracks make-up tests and gathers the term grades.

Lesson guide · bilingual program for children

tool

A step-by-step guide to use during class on a tablet: warm-up, activities, songs and wrap-up, one step at a time, with the audio tracks ready to play. A preparation tab shows, days in advance, what each lesson needs, like paint, flashcards or a mystery box.
